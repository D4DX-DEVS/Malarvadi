# Look Mechanics

The Malarvadi Guide Boy is a soft 3D child mascot with a flexible head and expressive eyes. Keep the feet, backpack, torso, and lower-body anchor stable while the eyes lead each direction and the head follows with a subtle turn or tilt. Use whole-eye/head movement rather than pupil-only motion so the look reads at sprite size. Preserve the front-facing body and avoid rotating the full sprite as a shortcut.
